BACKGROUND

Research meets engineering.

I received my master's degree from Nankai University, advised by Bo Ren and Ming-Ming Cheng. Before that, I received my bachelor's degree from Hebei University of Technology, advised by Haiyong Chen.

My earlier work explored visual inspection of solar cells; today I focus on the geometry and appearance of 3D worlds.
